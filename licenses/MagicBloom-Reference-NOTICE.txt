Magic Bloom reference / Magic Bloom 参考

The built-in DXL bloom references the broad, multi-scale glow approach in
Magic Bloom by luluco250. The DXL compute pyramid, smooth threshold and controls
are independently implemented; its automatic exposure/adaptation and lens-dirt
features are not included. This notice retains the reference author's license.

DXL 内置 Bloom 参考了 luluco250 的 Magic Bloom 的宽范围、多尺度柔光思路。
DXL 的计算着色器金字塔、柔和阈值和参数控制为独立实现，未接入其自动曝光、
自适应亮度或镜头污渍功能。此处保留参考实现作者的许可。

Source:
https://github.com/crosire/reshade-shaders/blob/bcb5ba54199f4455026dd8ba66dc1b74461d3152/Shaders/MagicBloom.fx
